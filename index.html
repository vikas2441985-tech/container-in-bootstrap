Fruits


Mango

Mango is a delicious tropical fruit known as the king of fruits. It grows during the summer season on large, leafy trees. Ripe mangoes have a sweet, juicy pulp and come in vibrant shades of yellow, orange, and red, while raw mangoes are green and sour. It is also the national fruit of India, where people love enjoying it fresh or in shakes, juices, and pickles.

blue berry

The blueberry is a small, round fruit known for its deep blue color and sweet, slightly tangy taste. Growing on low shrubs, these tiny berries are often called a "superfood" because they are packed with vitamins and antioxidants that keep our body and brain healthy. Blueberries are delicious to eat fresh by the handful and are also a popular ingredient in muffins, pancakes, and smoothies.

apple

The apple is a popular, round fruit known for its crisp texture and sweet or tangy taste. Growing on deciduous trees around the world, apples come in shades of red, green, and yellow. Packed with fiber and vitamins, this nutritious fruit lives up to the famous saying, "an apple a day keeps the doctor away." Apples are delicious to eat fresh and are also widely used to make juice, pies, and sauces.
